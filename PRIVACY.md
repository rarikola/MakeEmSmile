# MakeEmSmile privacy policy

_Last updated: November 5, 2026_

MakeEmSmile is a party game: a friend tries to make the player smile within one minute while the
phone's camera watches the player's face.

## What the app uses

- **Camera.** The camera is used only while the app is open, to measure facial expressions
  (smiling, raised eyebrows, yawning and similar) during a round.
- **Player names.** Names typed in for the scoreboard stay on the phone.

## What happens to your data

- All face analysis happens **on the phone**. Camera images and face measurements are **never
  uploaded**, sent to us, or shared with anyone by the app.
- The app does not record video or save photos. A single still frame from the end of a round is
  kept in memory to show on the results screen and is discarded when the next round starts or the
  app closes.
- If you tap **Share**, the app creates a result picture (including that frame) and hands it to the
  app you choose, such as a messenger. What happens after that is up to you and that app.
- The app stores only small game settings on the phone: the last high score with its player name,
  which camera you prefer, and which joke was shown last. Uninstalling the app deletes them.
- No accounts, no advertising, no analytics, no tracking, and no data is sold or shared.

## Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.
